DSH Remote Companion is a native iPhone, iPad and Mac companion for DeepSeek Harness (DSH), the self-hosted AI agent harness. Keep an eye on your running agent sessions without sitting in front of your computer.

FOLLOW YOUR AGENTS LIVE
• See every active session and its status at a glance
• Watch tool calls as they happen: file edits, commands, generation progress
• Get notified when a session is waiting for your answer

ON YOUR LOCK SCREEN
• Live Activities and Dynamic Island show the running turn
• Home Screen and Lock Screen widgets summarize your sessions
• Mac widgets in Notification Center

PAIR IN SECONDS
• Scan the QR code shown by the DSH web panel, or type the 6-character code
• Each device gets its own token, stored in the Apple Keychain
• Revoke a lost device from the DSH panel without affecting the others

PRIVATE BY DESIGN
• The app talks only to your own machine, over your local network or your private Tailscale network
• No account, no cloud relay, no analytics, no tracking
• Read-only by default: sending prompts or cancelling a turn requires a write-enabled token that you explicitly issue

REQUIREMENTS
DSH Remote Companion needs a computer running DeepSeek Harness with the DSH Remote host plugin installed. The plugin is open source (MIT) and documented on GitHub.

Available in English and French.
